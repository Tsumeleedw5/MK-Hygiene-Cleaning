Who We Are

We're Not Just a Cleaning Company

MK Hygiene & Cleaning Services was built on one simple belief: every space deserves to be properly clean. Based in Johannesburg CBD, we serve homes and businesses right across Gauteng — bringing professional-grade cleaning to places that matter to our clients.

Our team is trained, reliable and thorough. We understand that inviting a cleaning service into your home or workplace means trusting them to do the job right. That trust isn't something we take lightly — which is why we show up prepared, work with care, and don't leave until the job is done.

We also believe that professional cleaning shouldn't cost a fortune. Our pricing is competitive, honest and transparent — no hidden fees, no surprises.
